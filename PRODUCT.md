# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners, admins, and managers of a single Avito-based resale business. They work in short operational sessions: checking recent orders, creating orders manually, tracking returns, logging expenses, syncing product nomenclature from Avito, and reviewing profit.

## Product Purpose

CRM Avito tracks the operational cycle from order creation and supplier purchase through shipping, return handling, expenses, and financial reporting. Success means the user can quickly see what changed, what needs action, and how much profit the business actually made without importing Avito orders automatically.

## Brand Personality

Practical, precise, composed. The interface should feel like a serious mobile-first work tool: dense enough for repeated use, calm enough for financial work, and more intentional than a generic Tailwind dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboard markers: indigo-violet gradients, decorative blobs, emoji as UI icons, identical shadowed cards everywhere, hover effects on every row, weak gray-on-gray text, thin hairline borders, and placeholder SaaS copy. Do not turn the CRM into an art project or a marketing landing page.

## Design Principles

Show operational priority before decoration.

Make money, status, and tracking data optically stronger than labels.

Use color as semantics: status, profit/loss, category, and primary action should not collapse into one accent.

Keep mobile density high, but preserve tap targets and clear section rhythm.

Prefer restrained, durable product UI over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Preserve keyboard focus styles, avoid relying on color alone, use vector icons instead of emoji, and respect reduced-motion preferences for any animation.
